## How Can We Be So Wrong?
- Heuristics
- Confirmation Bias
---
### Heuristics
- Representiveness
"Assuming rare cases occur more frequently or vice-versa"
e.g. beards, slot machines
- Availability
"More known, available examples"
e.g. shark attacks vs malaria
- Anchoring and adjustment
"Tendency to use a given starting point (anchor), as the basis for a subsequent judgement"
"anchor is usually a number"
e.g. sales, price points
---
### Confirmation Bias
Active process:
- confirm our intuition
- disregard contradictions
---
